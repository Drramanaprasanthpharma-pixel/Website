Step 02

Dosage calculation based on body weight

Used whenever a drug is prescribed per kilogram of patient weight — common in paediatrics, anticoagulants, and weight‑banded antibiotics.

Formula Required dose = Patient's weight (kg) × Prescribed dose (per kg)

Step 03

Drug dosage calculation from stock solution

Works out how much of a stock (available) solution to draw up in order to deliver the strength the prescription actually asks for.

Formula Volume required = Strength required (mg) Stock strength (mg) × Volume of stock solution (mL)
Volume required —mL
Step 04

Calculation of flow rate (mL/h) for infusion pump

The setting you dial into an infusion pump — total volume to be given, divided by the time it should run over.

Formula Rate (mL/hr) = Volume (mL) Time (hr)
Pump rate —mL/hr
Step 05

Calculation of intravenous drip rate (gtts/min)

For gravity‑fed sets without a pump, counted in drops per minute against the giving set's drop factor.

Formula Flow rate (gtts/min) = Volume (mL) Time (hr) × Drop factor (gtts/mL)

To land on an accurate drops‑per‑minute figure, the calculator below converts your time into minutes first — set the toggle to match how you're entering it: gtts/min = (Volume × Drop factor) ÷ Time in minutes.

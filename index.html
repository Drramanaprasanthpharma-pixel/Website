Why compatibility matters

Two safe drugs can still be a dangerous mix

Y-site and syringe incompatibilities are a leading cause of preventable IV medication errors. Precipitation can block a line or embolize; degradation can silently rob a patient of an effective dose.

  • Physical incompatibility often shows as precipitate, haze, or color change in the line.
  • Chemical incompatibility can happen with no visible sign at all.
  • pH, concentration, diluent, and contact time all affect the outcome.
  • When in doubt, flush the line and administer separately.

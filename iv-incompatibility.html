What each severity rating means

Every result is graded so you can decide next steps in seconds. Always confirm against your facility's own protocol.

Compatible

These medications can typically be co-administered through the same line without a physical or chemical reaction.

Caution / Conditional

Compatibility may depend on concentration, diluent, or contact time — check the notes and consider a flush between doses.

Incompatible

Combining these drugs may cause precipitation, degradation, or loss of potency. Administer separately with a line flush.

⚠ Calcium incompatibility

Do not reconstitute or co-administer Ceftriaxone simultaneously with calcium-containing solutions (including via the same IV line), in any patient population, due to risk of fatal precipitation.

Clinical verification required. This tool is a bedside reference aid only and does not replace clinical judgment.

Enter height to enable ideal / adjusted body weight (Devine formula).
Cockcroft-Gault: CrCl (mL/min) = [(140 − age) × weight(kg) × (0.85 if female)] / (72 × SCr mg/dL)
Devine IBW: Male = 50 + 2.3 × (height in. − 60) · Female = 45.5 + 2.3 × (height in. − 60)
AdjBW: IBW + 0.4 × (ABW − IBW), used when ABW > ~120% of IBW

Clinical use note: Cockcroft-Gault estimates renal function for drug dosing purposes and does not equal measured GFR. Use actual body weight for most patients, ideal body weight for underweight patients, and adjusted body weight for obese patients (typically >120–130% of IBW), per your institution's protocol. Always confirm dose adjustments against the specific drug's renal dosing reference and your facility's AMS guidance.
